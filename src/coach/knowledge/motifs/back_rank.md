# Back-Rank Weakness

A castled king trapped behind its own pawns can be mated by a single rook or queen on the last rank.

- Make luft (h3/h6 or g3/g6) once heavy pieces face your back rank.
- Deflect or overload the defenders of the back rank to convert the motif.
- Trades that remove your last back-rank defender lose games — count defenders before recapturing.
