# Mating Patterns

Recognizing standard mates turns long calculations into instant pattern recall.

- Core patterns: back-rank mate, smothered mate, Greek gift (Bxh7+), Anastasia's and Arabian mate.
- A mating attack needs three things: an exposed king, attackers outnumbering defenders, and open lines.
- When you see a familiar pattern shape, calculate the forcing moves first (checks, captures, threats).
