# Prophylaxis

Prophylaxis is preventing the opponent's plan before pursuing your own — the move that answers "what do they WANT?" rather than "what do I want?" It is the defining habit of strong positional players.

**Why it matters:** Half of chess is the opponent's ideas. Players who never ask what the opponent intends lose to plans they watched being assembled for ten moves.

**Recognize it:** Prophylactic moments: they've massed pieces behind a pawn break; a knight is two hops from an outpost; your intended plan leaves one square fatally weak; every "automatic" move you both know is coming (their …f5, your b4). Signature prophylactic moves look mysterious: a3, h3, Kh1, Rb1 — each denies something specific.

**Common mistakes:**
- Playing prophylaxis against ghosts — preventing things the opponent can't actually do wastes the same tempo it saves.
- Pure passivity dressed as prophylaxis: prevention must serve or at least not obstruct your own plan.
- Asking "what do they threaten?" (tactics, one move) but never "what do they WANT?" (plans, five moves) — prophylaxis targets the second.

**How to think:** Before each of your turns, spend ten seconds playing the opponent's side: if given three free moves, what would they do? If the resulting position frightens you, your next move should interfere with it — remove the plan's key square, trade its key piece, or beat it on the clock with a faster threat of your own.

**Example:** Karpov's a4-a5 against queenside minority ideas, or the universal Kh1 before f4 in Sicilian structures — stepping off the g1–a7 diagonal BEFORE the tactics along it exist. Nothing is threatened; three future problems just quietly disappeared.

**Coach's tip:** When you can't find a good move, find their best plan and subtract it. "Improving my worst piece" and "preventing their best idea" together cover nearly every quiet position.

**Train it:** In your next three games, write down the opponent's plan (in words) every five moves. Accuracy of the guess matters less than the habit of asking.
