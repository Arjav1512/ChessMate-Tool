# Improving Under 1200

Below 1200, games are decided by free material: pieces hung in one move and simple tactics missed. The improvement recipe is narrow and dramatic — safety first, tactics second, everything else later.

**Why it matters:** Study time is scarce; spending it where games are actually decided at YOUR level multiplies its value. Under 1200, one skill (not losing pieces) outweighs the entire rest of chess theory.

**Recognize it:** Signs this advice is for you: games regularly end by simple piece loss; you sometimes miss that a piece was attacked at all; opponent one-move threats surprise you; openings feel like memorized dance steps that stop mattering by move 10 (they do).

**Common mistakes:**
- Studying opening lines — at this level openings decide almost nothing; principles (develop, castle, don't hang things) cover 100% of your needs.
- Playing only blitz/bullet: speed cements exactly the impulsive habits you're trying to break.
- Solving advanced puzzles while skipping the basics: mate-in-one, one-move captures, and "is anything hanging?" drills are the actual curriculum.

**How to think:** One habit above all: before EVERY move, two checks — "what does their last move attack?" and "after my move, can anything of mine be taken for free?" Ten seconds, every move, both questions. Add basic opening principles (center pawn, knights and bishops out, castle by move 10) and endgame basics (how to mate with a queen; push the extra pawn) — that's the whole program.

**Example:** A typical under-1200 game turns on move 18: a bishop moves to attack something and steps off the defense of a knight — gone in one move. Not a tactic, not a combination: a skipped safety check. Fixing THAT pattern is worth 200 points; no opening study returns anything close.

**Coach's tip:** Play 15+10 or slower, always. You cannot practice a thinking routine in a time format that punishes thinking.

**Train it:** Daily: 10 minutes of simple tactics (mate-in-1, hanging pieces). Weekly: 2-3 slow games with the two-question ritual, then one review pass finding every piece either side hung. That's it — resist adding more until 1200.
