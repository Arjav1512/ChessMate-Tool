# London System (1.d4 with Bf4)

A sturdy setup-based opening: d4, Nf3, Bf4, e3, c3, Bd3 against almost anything.

- White's ideal structure aims the f4 bishop and a Ne5 outpost at the kingside.
- Black equalizes with early ...c5 and queenside pressure on b2.
- Danger for White: playing moves by rote when the position demands concrete play.
