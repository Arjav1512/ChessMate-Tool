# King's Indian Defense (1.d4 Nf6 2.c4 g6)

Black allows a big White center, then attacks it with pawn breaks and piece pressure.

- The classical race: Black storms the kingside with ...f5–f4–g4; White breaks with c5/b4 on the queenside.
- The dark-squared bishop on g7 is Black's soul — avoid blocking or trading it cheaply.
- Timing of ...e5 and ...f5 defines most middlegame plans.
