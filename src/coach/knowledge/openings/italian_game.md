# Italian Game (1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bc4)

Classical development aimed at the f7 square and a quick fight for the center.

- Slow plans (d3, c3, Re1, Nbd2–f1–g3) build a long maneuvering game.
- Watch the d4 break for White and the ...d5 break for Black.
- Early tactics revolve around f7/f2 and pins on the a2–g8 diagonal.
