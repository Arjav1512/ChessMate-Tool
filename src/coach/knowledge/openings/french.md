# French Defense (1.e4 e6)

A solid counterattacking defense: Black concedes space but strikes back at the d4/e5 pawn chain.

- The thematic breaks are ...c5 and ...f6 against White's pawn chain.
- Black's light-squared bishop is the problem piece — plan its future early.
- White chooses between advance (e5), exchange, and tension (Nc3/Nd2) setups.
