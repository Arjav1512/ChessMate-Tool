# Sicilian Defense (1.e4 c5)

Black fights for the center asymmetrically, trading the c-pawn for a central pawn and winning the half-open c-file.

- Black plays for counterattack, not equality — queenside play down the c-file is the main plan.
- White usually attacks in the center or on the kingside (Open Sicilian: d4, Nxd4).
- Typical Black errors: falling behind in development while grabbing pawns, allowing e4–e5 breaks.
