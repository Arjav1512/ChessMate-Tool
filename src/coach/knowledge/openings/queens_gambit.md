# Queen's Gambit (1.d4 d5 2.c4)

White offers the c-pawn to deflect Black from the center; it is a positional gambit, not a sacrifice.

- Declined (…e6): the Carlsbad structure and minority attack are core plans.
- Accepted (…dxc4): White regains the pawn and plays for a strong center with e4.
- Black must solve the c8 bishop; White must watch the ...c5 and ...e5 breaks.
