# Ruy Lopez (1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bb5)

White pressures the e5 pawn indirectly and plays for a small, lasting edge.

- The main-line plans: c3+d4 center, the Spanish maneuver Nb1–d2–f1–g3, kingside expansion.
- Black counters with ...b5, ...Na5/...Nb8 regrouping, or the open ...Nxe4 lines.
- Exchanging on c6 trades structure damage for the bishop pair.
