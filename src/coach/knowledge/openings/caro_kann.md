# Caro-Kann Defense (1.e4 c6)

Black prepares ...d5 while keeping the c8 bishop's diagonal open — solidity first.

- Compared to the French, the light-squared bishop develops outside the pawn chain.
- Typical plans: ...Bf5/...Bg4, ...e6, ...Nd7 and a timely ...c5 break.
- White's tries: Advance (e5) with kingside space, or classical Nc3 development.
