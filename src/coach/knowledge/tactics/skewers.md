# Skewers

A skewer is a reversed pin: the more valuable piece is in front and must move, exposing the one behind.

- Long-range pieces (queen, rook, bishop) deliver skewers along ranks, files, and diagonals.
- King+queen and king+rook alignments are the most common victims — avoid same-line placements.
- In endgames, the rook skewer behind a promoting pawn is a fundamental drawing/winning motif.
